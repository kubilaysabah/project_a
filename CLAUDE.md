## What this project is

**Working title: "project_a" — THE FINAL NAME IS NOT DECIDED YET (see "Name decision" below).**

A first-person **anomaly-loop horror** game ("The Exit 8" formula) set in a 1994 Turkish apartment building ("Huzur Apartmanı"). The player is the building's night janitor (kapıcı): climb floor by floor collecting trash bags; every landing is identical; if the landing is NORMAL continue up, if there is an ANOMALY turn back; a wrong call resets you to floor 1; reach floor 8 without a mistake → rooftop → morning → ending twist (the building is seen from outside to have NINE floors).

- **Goal:** ship on Steam in 2-3 months at $4.99. This is Şahan's quick-ship title (GAME #2 in the Moguls two-developer split — Moguls' CLAUDE.md explicitly does NOT track this project; this repo is its own board and memory).
- **Full concept document:** the user's `KAPICI-oyun-fikri.txt` (kept outside the repo); its content is summarized in this file and in the GitHub issues.
- **Engine:** Unity 6.5 (6000.5.5f1), URP (URP template with PC/Mobile render pipeline assets), new Input System. Project lives at the repo root. Each developer's local clone path, hardware and personal preferences live in that developer's own **`CLAUDE.local.md`** (gitignored, auto-loaded by Claude Code) — never in this file.

## Architecture decisions (agreed, mirrors Moguls principles)

- **PROGRESSION REDESIGN IN PROGRESS (#50, Şahan's decision 2026-09-26).** After playing No70 and Phasmophobia Şahan found the game had no story and no sense of finding things and moving on. Decision: the **"wrong floor → turn back, penalty" rule is dropped**. The game becomes a story-driven climb: the building is dark and the janitor carries the previous janitor's **headlamp**; on every floor exactly one flat is open (3+1, the same plan on every floor, dressed differently), inside it a **lock chain** (keys, code padlocks, fuses, hinge screwdriver) that grows 1-1-2-2-2-3-3-3 across the eight floors and ends with the floor key; a locked iron **floor door** opens with it; stairwell chores stay as the reason to enter the flat; the anomaly cards become a scripted **event library**; the roof key ends the game; music never stops (#49); autosave per floor. Premise: three previous janitors quit because of the building; the last one vanished. Full design with the floor-by-floor table and the remaining open decisions: `Docs/hikaye.md`. Estimated 5-6 weeks; Şahan informs Kubilay himself.

- **The old rule is GONE (#59, 2026-09-27; Şahan: "anomali mantığına artık yer yok").** Removed: `KatSayaci.KararVer`/penalty/`TurBitti` (the counter now only holds, loads and `Ilerle()`s the floor), `AnomaliSecici`, the 65 random cards in `Resources/Anomaliler`, the delayed lamp-death penalty, the unfinished-trash penalty, the anomaly journal (#45 shelved: `Defter`, `AnomaliDefteri`, `DefterGostergesi`, the menu's Journal page, `defter.*`/`anomali.*` strings), `GorunurlukOlcer` + `AnomaliGorunurlukDenetimi` + `Docs/anomali-gorunurluk.md`, `KatYoneticisi.eskiKural`/`testKarti`. Kept: `AnomaliUygulayici`/`AnomaliKarti` for a floor file's scripted landing card (`KatZinciriVarligi.sahanlikKarti`, today only floor 7), and `AnomaliButunlukTestleri` now checks every card in `Assets/Zincirler`. Architecture bullets below that describe turn-back, penalties, anomaly selection, the journal or the visibility audit are **historical**; do not rebuild them.

- **Inside a flat, rooms are locked too — progression is room to room.** Şahan's rule, repeated on 2026-09-27 so that all progression work is built on it: the player passes from one room to the next only with a **key**, a **code** or by **pulling a lever**; not every room opens (rooms outside the chain stay shut on that floor). Only the entrance hall, corridor and the chain's first room start open. The lever is the fifth lock type (#54, `Docs/hikaye.md` §6): pulling it somewhere unlocks a door elsewhere. The flat's `IcKapi` doors are the locks; #54 gives them types, #55 gives each floor its chain.

- **Finding gets harder floor by floor, and pieces interlock.** Şahan, 2026-09-27: keys and parts complete each other like a jigsaw (a broken key in pieces across rooms, a lock opened by a lever in another room), the important room does not open right away, and each floor is longer and more roundabout than the last (floor 5 has five locks; floors 6-8 at least as many, with more hidden spots and empty containers). `KilitTuru.KirikAnahtar` + `EsyaTuru.AnahtarParcasi` (pieces never enter the hand, saved per floor like drawing pieces, HUD counter via `KirikAnahtarlar`) and `KilitTuru.Ekipman` (several tools together) are the building blocks.

- **Every floor passes differently.** Şahan, 2026-09-27: besides mixing lock types, each floor has a signature mechanic — 1 key + break (tutorial), 2 sound-guided search (#62), 3 torn-note assembly (#63), 4 light and dark (#64), 5 mechanisms (#65), 6 ordering/placing + timed (#66, #58), 7 observation/anomaly + chase (#67, #58), 8 elevator (#68); table in `Docs/hikaye.md` §7. **Tools are kept separately**: a found tool (screwdriver) stays with the janitor like the headlamp, never occupies the hand and is never consumed; the one-item hand is for keys, fuses and notes.

- **The elevator and the roof key end the climb.** #68 (2026-09-27). `KilitTuru.Asansor` (appended): a fuse (`Gerekli`) goes into the landing's elevator panel (`Sahanlik_A/Asansor/Pano`, `AsansorPanosu`, three fuses, one missing, on every landing), the call button (`AsansorDugmesi`, "ÇAĞIR") brings the elevator from "9", the doors slide open on the lit `Asansor/Kabin`, and the lock on the cabin place is solved. Panel and button components are **persistent** (built and bound by the graybox, sounds referenced); the chain only calls `Hazirla`/`Sifirla`/`GelmisBaslat`, and `ZincirUygulayici.Temizle` destroys the cabin's `Kilit` immediately because `DaireyiTemizle` never reaches the landing. **The model's `Kapi_Sol` leaf stands on world +x** (Blender→Unity mirrors x): a leaf slides towards the side its renderer centre is on, never by its name. The frame (`Kasa`) has a `MeshCollider`; its old box collider covered the doorway. Floor 8's floor key is the **roof key**: under the new rule reaching the roof room is not a decision (`catiOdasinda`), `CatiKapisi` says KİLİTLİ/AÇ and calls `KatYoneticisi.CatiKapisiAnahtarlaAcildi`. The finale's ninth-floor window starts dark and `FinalSahnesi` lights it after a distant ding.

- **Each floor is saved at its start; a reload restarts it (#84, Şahan 2026-09-30).** Reaching a new floor saves the floor and the tool bag (`Kayit.KatBasiniKaydet`). Loading a save runs `Kayit.KatBasinaDon` before the flat is built: the floor's locks, pieces and moments are cleared and the tool bag returns to the floor-start snapshot, as if entering the floor for the first time. A save-less new game calls `Kayit.Sil()`. In-session behaviour is unchanged. The Esc "Katı Sıfırla" button (#82) was removed. **Floors page (#85):** title and Esc menus list floors 1-8 (`KatSecimi`, tested). - **Being caught by the chase restarts the floor, not the game.** Şahan, 2026-09-27 (#58, `99c03a0`): `KatYoneticisi.KacistaYakalandi` fades out, empties the hand, `Kayit.KatiSifirla(kat)` (the floor's locks and pieces; tools stay), puts the player at the landing start and rebuilds the flat; the fade lives on the manager because the rebuild destroys `KacisAni`. The silhouette walks 2.0 m/s (player 1.8).

- **The building has one entity; tension is a number the player's behaviour moves (#86, Şahan 2026-10-01).** After comparing with No70 and Phasmophobia Şahan found floors 1-5 had no threat. `GerilimKurali` (plain C#, tested) holds a 0-1 tension: it rises while the player is inside the flat with the headlamp on (0.0035/s × floor multiplier, a little more while walking), jumps on noise (`Varlik.Gurultu`: jar 0.25, wrong door 0.3, hinge door falling 0.3, lever 0.1, locked rattle 0.04, drawer 0.02), and falls on the landing (0.003/s), with the lamp off or crouched (0.004/s) and fast when crouched + lamp off + still (0.02/s). Levels: 0.30 → lamps flicker, footsteps above, music swell; 0.60 → `GezenGolge.Belir()`, door knock, headlamp dies briefly; 0.85 → the **hunt** (`Varlik`, on the manager object like `GezenGolge`): after 2.5 s of footsteps the Gölge spawns on the oldest off-screen point of the player's last 12 s trail (or behind the player) and follows the trail at 2.0 m/s × multiplier, opening closed room doors it passes. Caught (0.8 m) → `KatYoneticisi.AvdaYakalandi` (floor start, **locks kept**, hand emptied). Escaping: the floor door opens, or the player is **hidden** (crouched, lamp off — a hidden player leaves no trail) and the Gölge reaches the end of the trail without seeing them for 2.5 s; 18 s timeout. Floor multipliers live in `GerilimKurali.KatinCarpani` (1: 0.6 and no hunt, 8: 1.3). The rule pauses during the floor-6 hide moment, the floor-7 chase, menus and the opening; `Varlik.Kapali` (static) switches it off for `OyunBotu` and `KatDenetimi`. Tension is never shown in the game UI (the dev overlay prints it). Noise sources only call `Varlik.Gurultu(miktar)`; they know nothing about the entity. `GezenGolge`'s own 60-120 s timer (#71) was **removed** on Şahan's decision (`e9406f6`): the shadow appears only through tension (level 2 → `GezenGolge.HaberciyleBelir`: footsteps above, then the shadow) or a wrong-door knock on floor 7. `GolgeZamanlayici` and its tests are gone.

- **What a landing shows is derived, never pushed.** `KatYoneticisi` runs in `LateUpdate` with `[DefaultExecutionOrder(100)]`, i.e. after `SonsuzMerdiven`, so on the frame the player reaches B they are teleported first and the decision and landing refresh happen in the same frame — the first rendered image is already the new floor.
